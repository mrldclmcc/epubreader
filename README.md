# EPUB Reader

This is a simple EPUB reader that runs entirely in the web browser. You do not need to install any software or create an account. All your book files stay on your computer and are never sent to the internet.

## What this tool does

This tool lets you open and read EPUB books on any device with a modern web browser. It shows your book with clean, comfortable text that is easy on the eyes. You can change the look and feel of the reader to match your personal reading preferences.

## Features

- Works completely offline after the page loads. Your books are processed only on your own device.
- Clean and calm reading view with well‑spaced text and elegant typography.
- Light and dark colour themes that respect your system preference or you can switch manually.
- Adjust the text size, line height, and column width to make reading more comfortable.
- Choose from different font styles, including a special font for readers with dyslexia.
- Hide all images with one click if you prefer a text‑only reading experience.
- Table of contents that is generated automatically from your book.
- Navigate easily using the buttons at the top and bottom of the page, or use your keyboard.
- Your reading progress is saved automatically. If you close the page and come back to the same book, you will continue from where you stopped.
- All your settings (theme, font, size, and more) are remembered for your next visit.

## How to use the reader

1. Open the HTML file in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, or any modern browser).
2. You will see a screen asking you to upload an EPUB file.
3. Drag and drop your EPUB file onto the dashed box, or click the button to select a file from your computer.
4. Wait a moment while the book is prepared. The reader will then open automatically.
5. Use the left and right arrow buttons at the top or bottom of the page to move between sections.
6. Click the "Contents" button to open the table of contents and jump to any chapter.
7. Click the "Options" button to open the settings panel and adjust the look of your book.

## Keyboard shortcuts

If you prefer using the keyboard, these shortcuts are available:

- Left arrow key: go to the previous section
- Right arrow key: go to the next section
- Press the T key: open or close the table of contents
- Press the S key: open or close the settings panel
- Press the Escape key: close any open panel

## Settings explained

When you click the Options button, you can change these things:

- **Theme**: Switch between light and dark mode.
- **Text Size**: Make the letters larger or smaller.
- **Font Family**: Choose a different typeface for the text. Options include a classic serif font, simple sans‑serif fonts, and a font designed to help dyslexic readers.
- **Line Height**: Control the space between lines of text.
- **Column Width**: Control how wide the text column is on the screen. Narrower columns are often easier to read.
- **Show Images**: Uncheck this box to hide all pictures in the book.

## Privacy and security

This reader is completely private. No file is uploaded to any server. All processing happens inside your browser. Your books and reading progress are stored only on your own device. You can read any EPUB file without worrying about your data being collected.

## Requirements

- A modern web browser (released in the last few years).
- Any valid EPUB file that is not protected by digital rights management (DRM).
